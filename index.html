I am a CS PhD student at Stony Brook University (SBU) . My research interests include (but are not limited to) natural language processing, agentic ai, efficient reasoning, and explainable AI.

Prior to joining SBU, I worked three years as a Machine Learning Software Engineer at Goava, a Swedish B2B AI startup. I completed my undergraduate degree in CS from Shahjalal University of Science and Technology (SUST). While at SUST, I was an active Competitive Programmer at SUST ACM Lab where I solved mathematical and real-world programming problems. I was lucky to have Dr. Md. Ruhul Amin, Sabir Ismail, Dr. Sudipta Kar and Md. Saiful Islam advise my undergraduate thesis. My thesis focused on ‘Predicting & Interpreting the Likeability of Books’.

In addition, I worked on multiple projects as a remote Research Assistant (RA) under the supervision of Dr. Md. Ruhul Amin (FAIR Lab, Fordham University), and afterwards with Dr. Sheikh Rabiul Islam and Dr. Shubhra Kanti Karmaker (Santu) (BDI Lab, Auburn University).

Apart from these, I love to play table tennis and badminton.
