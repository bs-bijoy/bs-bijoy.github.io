Biddut Sarker Bijoy

I am a Software Engineer at Goava AB, a Sweden-based AI startup for B2B sales. Prior to joining Goava AB, I completed my BS in Computer Science from Shahjalal University of Science and Technology (SUST). During my studies, I got opportunities to work as a Remote Research Assistant with Dr. Md. Ruhul Amin (FAIR Lab, Fordham University), and afterwards with Dr. Sheikh Rabiul Islam and Dr. Shubhra Kanti Karmaker (Santu) (BDI Lab, Auburn University) from Q2 2020 to Q1 2022.

Throughout my 3 years as a Competitive Programmer at SUST's ACM Lab - 332, I have learned various Core and Advanced Algorithms, Data Structures, and had the chance to participate in various national and inter-university programming contests.

I was lucky to have Dr. Md. Ruhul Amin, Sabir Ismail, Dr. Sudipta Kar and Md. Saiful Islam advise my undergraduate's Thesis. My work surrounded Predicting & Interpreting the Likelihood of Books.
